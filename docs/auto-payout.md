# Automatic payout history and retry
Official source: https://developers.trendyol.com/docs/cari-hesap-ekstresi-entegrasyonu . Payment order lists PAID orders, max 10 per page; detail queries are by paymentOrderId and may lag payout creation. Every automated list pass refreshes page 0 and at most one historical page. A successful historical pass advances a tenant/seller cursor; failure retains it; completion wraps to revisit shifting offset pagination. This is bounded coverage, not a guaranteed complete snapshot. Each page is validated and atomically stored. Changed payout amount or day clears prior reconciliation and retry state. Detail failures invalidate the old summary and use per-payment exponential backoff from 30 minutes up to one day, allowing other payouts to proceed. The store-level job may still retry on its normal schedule. No bank integration or independent bank verification, no write to Trendyol. Existing bank receipts are retained and compared against the newly observed amount. Schema 8. Synthetic tests; live merchant confirmation pending.
