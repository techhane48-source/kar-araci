# Observed store financial deductions
Official documentation: https://developers.trendyol.com/docs/cari-hesap-ekstresi-entegrasyonu and https://developers.trendyol.com/reference/getotherfinancials . Stoppage includes withholding and cancellations. DeductionInvoices can be filtered by PlatformServiceFee. The dashboard displays observed store-level transaction-date debit, credit, net and query coverage separately from order-date profit estimates. A credit reduces observed deduction. Missing records are not treated as zero. Excluded scope or conflicting signs are visible. A filtered service-fee row with a conflicting explicit subtype or known cargo label is rejected; old classification flags are cleared on document upsert. No assumption of a per-package invoice allocation or independently confirmed VAT. Withholding is an advance tax/cash deduction, not automatically an operating expense. Current 13.19 TL order service estimate is still not a verified current tariff. No double subtraction or claim of final profit. Live validation pending.
