# Campaign evidence and limitations
Source: user-provided Trendyol Akademi Plus Commission Tariffs lesson (11 examples), Flash Products lesson, original four XLSX layouts inspected 2026-11-27. No merchant rows or identifiers are included here.
- Customer price subtracts seller and platform discounts; commission basis subtracts only seller-funded discount. The Plus-specific price path receives general promotions only; the normal-price Plus-promotion path receives eligible general and Plus promotions. Lower customer price wins; differing bases on ties are not resolved by the lesson.
- Plus rate first, then normal product tariff on the Plus basis, then standard rate; input tariff coverage must be explicit. Direct Plus price must not exceed normal TSF or offered limit.
- XLSX formula tier validation is not identical to realized commission calculation. Normal template tests entered TSF, Plus template tests entered Plus price. Do not reverse infer promotion funding by current basis/TSF ratio.
- Exact readers for Plus dual period, star tiers and Flash slots. Repeated Flash barcode rows are separate offers. Current rate is not a guaranteed new-price rate. No automatic price recommendations or marketplace writes for these three modules yet. Unknown promotion, tariff or basket rules block calculation.
- The pure rules engine uses explicitly evaluated discount amounts, not inferred free text. Connecting actual campaign contributions and matching effective-date tariff evidence across uploads, plus per-module original-workbook exports and verification of panel acceptance, remain separate tasks. The lesson does not specify equal-price ties with different funding, percent stacking/rounding, or all cart conditions.
